# UI117 — HR and personal records

Wavelink, AJ Offshore Solutions; core **1.34.19**. Parent is exact uploaded UI116 main `1f1f75401f9c404cb5859de3b1a3742af2e60010`, verified live on Sulmara and the client demo.

Open **HR & personal records** under People, or **My HR records** on your own profile. **Personal certificates & documents** includes personal medicals, training, passport, seaman’s book and visa records. **Inventory certificates** continues to cover inventory/equipment. Their records, files and permissions are separate. Employee records are private to their owner and explicitly authorised HR users, per area.

Administrators grant the **HR / personal records** flags through Administration → People → Company permissions. `hr.view_own` opens the personal workspace. Own document, timesheet and expense submission rights are separate. Document management, travel management, crew assignment management and timesheet/expense review cover the company and must be granted independently. Supervisor/department-head titles and the All operational access preset do not add company-wide HR rights. Old explicit account policies deny absent new keys; grant the needed employee flags. Inherited ordinary role defaults include own service rights. HR does not grant company administration or change existing membership approval.

Save documents with supplied issue/expiry information, explicit no-expiry or unknown-expiry state and reminder window (90 days initially). Attach unchanged PDF/JPEG/PNG evidence: five MiB per file, six active files per record. UTC date status supplies Due soon/Expired alerts to the HR overview and bell’s HR category. This records dates rather than certificate authenticity or travel/work eligibility. Optional HR email starts off: use personal Email alerts settings, a verified mailbox and the existing configured company mail service. Existing category choices and quiet-hours settings remain preserved; email summaries contain counts and a link.

HR travel records hold dates, optional clocks with explicit time zone, route/reference/accommodation/status. Dated crew assignments hold person, vessel/location, department, joining/leaving dates and optional shift window. They do not book tickets or silently replace current handover cycles and members.

Timesheets hold up to 31 distinct dates in a period of up to 31 days, exact two-decimal hours, work locations and activities. Expenses hold a dated amount/currency, merchant/project/description and receipt. Draft/Returned forms are editable by their employee. Submit freezes the saved values and receipts. A separate authorised reviewer sees the actual submitted days/expense/receipt and approves or returns them; returning requires a comment. No self-review, payroll calculation, currency conversion or payment is inferred. Source-page controls retrieve older records without growing the displayed cards without limit; search and totals label their loaded page.

Save drafts before leaving. Unfinished forms can be closed and restored in this tab for the same account without replacement by another form. Restoring keeps the actual employee identity. Individual permission changes remove stale private content and suppress late files/responses while preserving unsent work. Lost-response saves offer Retry unchanged request with the original operation ID. Private HR editor values and file bytes are not placed in shared browser recovery storage. Reload/close warns if any retained HR form exists; save server drafts before closing the tab.

The five HR tables are created additively on compatible company storage. No existing inventory, handover, account, membership, company configuration or personal draft is reset. Full backups with HR evidence require administrator authority and include sensitive staff files/history. HR starts unchecked in selected-module exports; unrelated imports open separate projects and preserve the existing company. Keep UI117 or later when HR data exists.

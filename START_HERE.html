What UI78 changes
  • Manifests, saved shipment records, Receiving & placement, Assets & transit, asset detail and Item journey now own their final readable hierarchy directly.
  • Pale text and mixed cards inherited from older logistics styles are corrected without a post-render decorator.
  • Receiving refresh keeps the mounted route visible and adds no independent timer.
  • Wide asset tables scroll inside their card on phones while the document remains contained.
  • Existing dispatch, receipt, placement, quarantine and custody permission checks remain authoritative.
